At Akto Eiendom I was the link between the people who run the buildings, the suppliers who maintain them and the documentation that has to describe all of it. The job had two sides: making sure the day-to-day operations and maintenance actually happened, and making sure what happened was documented so the next person could find it.

## What the role covered

- Coordinating operations and maintenance across the properties: planning, following up suppliers and service agreements, and handling deviations.
- FDVU documentation for the portfolio: collecting, structuring and quality-assuring documentation from projects and suppliers, and keeping it up to date through changes.
- Reporting and internal routines, so operations, finance and management worked from the same picture.

## What I took with me

The practical side of FDVU: documentation is only worth something when the people who do the work can find it, trust it and keep it current. That is the thinking I brought to the restructuring work at [Boligbygg](/projects/fdvu-boligbygg-en/).
