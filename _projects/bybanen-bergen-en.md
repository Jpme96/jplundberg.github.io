The Bybanen facility is not an ordinary building. It is a depot, a workshop and a technical plant that the city's light rail depends on, and it is where I started working with operations and FDVU in 2017.

## What the role covered

- Daily operation of the building and its technical systems: ventilation, heating, power, doors and gates, fire safety, and the systems specific to the tram depot.
- Planned maintenance and follow-up of service agreements, with suppliers in and out of a facility that was always in use.
- The documentation behind it: drawings, instructions and service history, so faults could be found and fixed fast.

## What I learned

That operations is where the cost of poor documentation shows up first. When a system fails at night, the time it takes to find the right drawing or the right contact is time the trams don't run. It is why I have worked on documentation ever since.
