## The challenge

A large property portfolio depends on its FDV documentation: drawings, manuals, maintenance instructions and records for every building. When documents are missing, outdated or hard to find, maintenance takes longer and decisions are made on uncertain information.

## What I do

I lead the quality control of around 600,000 FDV documents across 400 buildings. For each building, the documentation is checked for completeness and correctness, and gaps are followed up so that the records operations and maintenance rely on can be trusted.

- Checking documents against requirements for content and structure
- Following up missing or outdated documentation
- Making documentation easier to find for the people who use it every day

## Result so far

Half of the portfolio has been quality controlled, about 300,000 documents. The work continues building by building towards a complete, reliable documentation base.
